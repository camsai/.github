# Contributing to CAMSAI

This file states the terms that apply across every CAMSAI repository. A
repository that needs more than this — how to build it, how it is tested, what
its review expectations are — says so in its own `CONTRIBUTING.md`, which takes
precedence for that project.

## Terms

-   **Licensing.** Code is released under the Apache License 2.0; documents,
    standards, and educational material under CC BY 4.0. By contributing, you
    agree that your contribution is licensed under the outbound license of the
    repository you are contributing to.
-   **Developer Certificate of Origin.** Contributions are accepted under the
    [DCO](https://developercertificate.org/): certify that you wrote the
    contribution, or otherwise have the right to submit it under the
    repository's license, by signing off your commits with `git commit -s`.
    CAMSAI does not require a contributor license agreement.
-   **Provenance.** Do not contribute code, data, or text that you do not have
    the right to distribute under the repository's license. Third-party
    material must be identified with its origin and license.
-   **Conduct.** All CAMSAI spaces are governed by the
    [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/camsai/governance/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## How decisions are made

Technical decisions belong to each project's maintainers. Decisions about the
consortium as a whole — admitting a project, changing how we govern ourselves —
are made by the Steering Committee under
[GOVERNANCE.md](https://github.com/camsai/governance/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md).

To propose something that spans the consortium rather than a single project,
open a pull request or an issue against
[camsai/governance](https://github.com/camsai/governance).
